# PARTY CHARACTERS

## SILAS — The Awakened

**Frame:** Human (living)
**Role:** Damage/Support Hybrid
**Age:** 23 (memories span centuries)

**Appearance:**
Pale skin, dark circles permanent beneath grey eyes. Hair prematurely white at the temples. Hands never stop moving—tracing patterns on surfaces, tapping rhythms only he hears. Wears a threadbare scholar's coat over salvaged armor plates.

**Backstory:**
Silas woke at fourteen screaming words in a language dead for three hundred years. The Awakened rarely remember more than fragments. Silas remembers *everything*—seventeen lives stacked like sediment. He knows how to forge blades he's never touched. Weeps at songs from extinct cultures. His most recent previous life ended badly: he was a Threadwright, helping souls escape the cycle. Someone betrayed him. He remembers the knife entering his back. Doesn't remember the face.

**Personality:**
Exhausted but driven. Speaks in half-finished sentences because he's holding back information from lives he shouldn't reference. Dry humor masks genuine terror—he knows what the Loom does to souls, and he's running out of lifetimes to fix it.

**Element Affinity:** Aetheric/Vital hybrid

**Signature Abilities:**

- Memory Surge — "I've killed you before."
- Vital Trace — Healing through past-life knowledge
- Bleedthrough — Channeling accumulated combat experience

-----

## CORRA — The Cordwork

**Frame:** Konstruk (Cordwork-class)
**Role:** Tank/Support
**Bound:** 8 years ago

**Appearance:**
Burlap skin stretched over a wooden armature. Face is a painted cloth mask—the painted smile doesn't move, but her button eyes somehow convey expression. Joints wrapped in stained bandages. Her left arm is newer than the rest; the stitching doesn't match. A faded blue ribbon tied around her neck—she won't say who gave it to her.

**Backstory:**
Corra doesn't remember agreeing to be Threaded. She woke in this Cordwork frame in a Threadwright workshop with her previous life's memories dissolving like sugar in rain. She knows she was a mother. She knows she had a daughter. She doesn't know the daughter's name anymore. The ribbon is all that's left. She works with the Threadwright Accord now, helping other Konstruks adjust, pretending she's adjusted herself.

**Personality:**
Gentle despite everything. Speaks slowly—Cordwork frames process language with delay. Fiercely protective of living party members; she's already lost one life, she won't watch others lose theirs. Occasionally freezes mid-sentence when a memory surfaces, then continues as if nothing happened.

**Element Affinity:** Vital

**Signature Abilities:**

- Interpose — Taking damage meant for allies
- Mend-Weave — Cordwork self-repair
- Thread-Hold — Drawing enemy attention

-----

## VESK — The Burner

**Frame:** Human (living)
**Role:** Damage Dealer
**Age:** 31

**Appearance:**
Burn scars web across his forearms—self-inflicted, from early training. Shaved head reveals more scarring at the base of his skull. Thermal-tapper's traditional red leather coat, sleeves cut short to vent heat. Eyes have a faint orange luminescence; side effect of channeling too much thermal ley. Carries a forging hammer converted for combat.

**Backstory:**
Vesk was Veinkeeper militia before he saw what they did to a Null Zone village—"purification," they called it. Burned the Loom-Blind alive to "return them to the cycle they'd escaped." He killed his commanding officer, took his coat, ran. Now he sells his tapping to whoever pays. Tells himself it's survival. Knows it's penance. The hammer belonged to his father, a smith who never hurt anyone.

**Personality:**
Blunt. Angry. Covers vulnerability with aggression. Respects Konstruks more than most living humans—"At least they're honest about what they are." Drinks too much. Fights too readily. But he'll walk through fire for people who earn his loyalty, and that's not metaphor.

**Element Affinity:** Thermal

**Signature Abilities:**

- Flare Strike — Concentrated fire damage
- Burnout Surge — Area attack that costs him
- Smolder — Lingering burn effect

## ECHO — The Hollow Voice

*Joins in Act 2*

**Frame:** Konstruk (Porcelain-class)
**Role:** Support/Debuff
**Bound:** Unknown (claims 200+ years)

**Appearance:**
Cracked white porcelain face, gold filigree tracing where the fractures spread. One eye is original—painted blue enamel. The other was replaced with a veinite crystal that glows faintly green. Moves with unsettling grace; porcelain frames were built for aristocratic ballrooms. Wears tattered finery from a noble house that no longer exists.

**Backstory:**
Echo shouldn't still be coherent. Porcelain frames weren't built to last centuries. She's Fraying—badly—and the voices of previous Threaded souls bleed through her speech. Sometimes mid-sentence she'll speak in a child's cadence, or a soldier's bark, or a language no one recognizes. She sought out Silas specifically. Claims to remember him. Claims to remember what he forgot. Won't say more until she trusts the party.

**Personality:**
Eerily calm. Speaks in riddles not by choice but because her thoughts arrive fragmented from multiple sources. Moments of startling clarity break through—usually with information no one should know. Doesn't fear destruction; fears what's left of her drifting into the Loom and losing the thread she's been holding for two hundred years.

**Element Affinity:** Aetheric/Vital

**Signature Abilities:**

- Fracture Song — Weakening enemies through discordant resonance
- Whisper-Touch — Group healing
- The Voice Before — Revealing hidden truths about enemies

# ENEMY COMPENDIUM

## Act 1 Enemies

### Ley-Rat Swarm

**Type:** Beast | **Element:** Vital

*Behavior:* Fast but fragile. Attacks lowest HP target.

*Lore:* Vermin drawn to veinite deposits. Their bites carry trace ley—enough to cause infection. Found wherever raw veinite bleeds into open spaces. Travel in chittering waves, eyes glowing faint green.

-----

### Frayed Cordwork

**Type:** Konstruk | **Element:** Vital

*Behavior:* Slow. Uses "Desperate Grab" to immobilize one party member.

*Lore:* Abandoned Konstruks whose souls degraded past coherence. They attack reflexively, no mind left behind the motions. Some still wear the clothes their masters dressed them in. Some still try to speak.

-----

### Veinkeeper Scout

**Type:** Human | **Element:** Thermal

*Behavior:* Uses "Sear" (thermal damage) and "Signal Flare" (calls reinforcement if not defeated quickly).

*Lore:* Low-ranking Veinkeeper militia. Young. Zealous. Following orders they don't question. Red leather uniforms. Thermal brands on their palms—marks of service.

-----

## Act 2 Enemies

### Node Wisp

**Type:** Elemental | **Element:** Aetheric

*Behavior:* Casts "Null Pulse" to drain LP from party pool.

*Lore:* Fragments of ley consciousness that coalesce near damaged Nodes. Not alive. Not dead. Just *process*. They flicker between visibility, shapes constantly shifting—almost faces, almost words.

-----

### Ironbound Deserter

**Type:** Konstruk | **Element:** None (Neutral)

*Behavior:* Heavy hitter. Uses "Siege Blow" periodically for massive damage.

*Lore:* Military Konstruks who walked away from their posts. Some seek peace. Some just want to break things that aren't themselves. Iron frames. Siege-class. Built for war, now purposeless.

-----

### Hollow March Remnant

**Type:** Konstruk | **Element:** Aetheric

*Behavior:* "Soul Scream" damages all party members and can inflict Confusion.

*Lore:* Hollow March members too Frayed to follow orders. Left behind when the main force moved on. They scream because it's all they remember how to do. Multiple voices compete for control of a single frame.

-----

## Act 3 Enemies

### Veinkeeper Warden

**Type:** Human | **Element:** Thermal

*Behavior:* Commands battlefield. "Judgment" hits hardest-hitting party member. "Purity Protocol" buffs all ally enemies.

*Lore:* True believers. They've seen the Veinwork's design and decided it must be protected at any cost. Senior rank. Ceremonial armor over practical plate. They've made peace with what they do.

-----

### Awakened Horror

**Type:** Aberration | **Element:** Vital

*Behavior:* "Memory Drown" forces single target to skip turn as they relive trauma.

*Lore:* What happens when too many lives stack wrong. The Awakened who couldn't hold the weight. Now they're everyone and no one, and they want company. Shapes constantly shifting—faces melting into other faces.

-----

## BOSS: The Assemblage (Fragment)

**Type:** Konstruk (Reliquary) | **Element:** All

*A piece of something larger. Nineteen souls threaded into a single Reliquary frame—the oldest, largest class of Konstruk. Each soul maintains partial consciousness. They don't agree. They can't agree. But they share one thing: rage at being forgotten.*

**Behavior Pattern:**

- "Nineteen Voices" — Random element attack, hits all party
- "It Should Hurt" — Massive single target damage
- "When We Scream" — Drains LP from party
- At half health: Gains "Resonance Shield" — Requires elemental rotation to break
- "The Thread That Binds" — Can temporarily turn fallen party member against the group

**Lore:**
This isn't the full Assemblage—that horror would end you in moments. This is a *piece*, sent to intercept Silas before he reaches the Node. It speaks in layered voices. Some of them beg for help. Some of them apologize. Most of them just scream.

# WORLD TERMINOLOGY

**The Loom:** The metaphysical system that processes souls after death, recycling them into new lives. Maintained by the Engineer.

**The Engineer:** The entity (god? machine? both?) that built and maintains the Loom. No one has spoken to it directly.

**Veinwork:** The physical manifestation of ley energy running through the world. Powers magic, maintains the cycle.

**Nodes:** Points where the Veinwork concentrates. Sacred sites. Heavily guarded.

**Konstruks:** Artificial bodies housing human souls. Various classes based on construction material and purpose.

**Fraying:** The degradation of a soul over time, especially in Konstruk frames. Eventually leads to complete incoherence.

**The Hollow March:** Rebel faction of Konstruks seeking to destroy the Loom entirely. Extremists.

**Veinkeepers:** Religious-military order sworn to protect the Veinwork. Fanatics in their own way.

**Threadwrights:** Underground network helping souls escape or navigate the cycle. Silas was one.

**Awakened:** Humans who retain memories of past lives. Rare. Often unstable.

**Null Zones:** Areas where ley energy fails. The Loom-Blind live here—people born outside the cycle.
